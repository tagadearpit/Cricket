About VCA Stadium

The Vidarbha Cricket Association (VCA) Stadium in Jamtha, Nagpur, is buzzing with excitement as it has been officially selected to host key matches for the IPL 2026 Playoffs. Known for its world-class infrastructure and its status as one of the largest cricket stadiums in India by field area, the venue is a favorite among players for its fair bounce and excellent floodlighting. Local authorities and the VCA have already begun preparations to manage the massive influx of fans, ensuring that the "Orange City" provides a spectacular atmosphere for the high-stakes Eliminator and Qualifier matches. This selection is a proud moment for Nagpur’s cricket enthusiasts, as it brings the thrill of post-season IPL action back to the heart of Vidarbha.

Why is this stadium special?

  • It has a massive seating capacity for thousands of fans.
  • It is known for having one of the best outfields in India.
  • The stadium features world-class corporate boxes and player facilities.
  • Excellent floodlighting and modern infrastructure make it ideal for night matches.
