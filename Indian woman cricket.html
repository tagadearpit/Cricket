About Indian Woman's Tour to South Africs 2026 : -

The India Women's Tour of South Africa 2026 is set to begin on April 17, 2026, consisting of a high-stakes five-match T20I series. This tour serves as a crucial final preparation for both teams before the ICC Women’s T20 World Cup in June. Led by captain Harmanpreet Kaur and vice-captain Smriti Mandhana, the Indian squad features a strong mix of experience and fresh talent, including a maiden call-up for young batter Anushka Sharma and the return of pacer Kashvee Gautam. The matches will be played across three iconic venues—Durban, Johannesburg, and Benoni—concluding on April 27. With India currently ranked third and South Africa fifth in the world, fans are expecting an intense battle as the "Women in Blue" look to maintain their dominant form following their recent 2-1 series win in Australia.

Tour details : -

The Indian Women's Team is traveling to South Africa for a 5-match T20I series starting on April 17, 2026. This tour is very important for the T20 World Cup preparation. The matches will be played at the following places:
